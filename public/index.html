Settings

Optional extras. Everything here is off unless you turn it on, and turning it off hides it again.

When Windows starts and after every 3-hour refresh, you get one notification listing what's due today and early tomorrow morning (before 10 AM). Nothing due → no notification. They work even when this page is closed, as long as the dashboard is running.

Notifications are blocked. Turn on “Notifications” (and “Windows PowerShell” further down), then send a test.

Not seeing them? Windows Settings → System → Notifications: make sure notifications are on and “Windows PowerShell” is allowed (the dashboard uses it to show them). Do Not Disturb hides pop-ups but they still land in the notification center.

Registrar courses for this term are shown automatically. Other Brightspace sites (orientation, clubs, trainings) appear only while they have deadlines in the next 30 days. Use these switches to override.
